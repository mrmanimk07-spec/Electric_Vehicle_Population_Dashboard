# Electric_Vehicle_Population_Dashboard
Analyzing Electric Vehicle Adoption Trends and Performance Across Regions

📌 Overview

A data analytics project focused on understanding Electric Vehicle (EV) adoption trends, geographic distribution, and market characteristics using registration data.

🎯 Goals

Analyze EV growth by model year

Compare BEV vs PHEV adoption

Identify top EV manufacturers and regions

Evaluate electric range and CAFV eligibility

📊 Data & Tools

Data: EV registration dataset (vehicle, range, type, geography, incentives)

Tools: Excel (cleaning, pivots), Power BI (DAX, dashboards)

🔍 Key Insights

EV adoption has increased sharply in recent years

BEVs dominate over PHEVs

EVs are concentrated in urban and coastal regions

Few manufacturers lead the market

Electric range improves with newer models

CAFV incentives strongly influence adoption

✅ Conclusion

The analysis reveals a rapid and sustainable shift toward electric mobility, supporting data-driven decisions for infrastructure planning, policy design, and market strategy.
